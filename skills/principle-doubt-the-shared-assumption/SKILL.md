# Doubt the Shared Assumption

When two attempts that share an assumption both fail, stop fixing. Write the assumption down, find an observation that could disprove it, and measure where the problem really concentrates.

**Why.** A third attempt on a wrong assumption costs as much as the first two and fails the same way.

**In practice.**
- Name the assumption explicitly.
- Pick the cheapest observation that would contradict it.
- Change whatever creates the problem, rather than compensating for it again.

**In the Mulligan loop.** Bring the assumption to the developer before a third attempt; it may be theirs to correct.

**Not when.** A first failure is just a failure.

**Check.** What did the failed attempts have in common?
